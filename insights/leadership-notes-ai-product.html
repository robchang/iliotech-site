Insight · 2026-11-01

Leadership notes for AI product orgs

AI product leadership is less about prompting cleverness and more about the same things that always mattered: clear outcomes, honest scope, and teams that can learn from production.

Hire for judgment under uncertainty

Models change weekly. Judgment about user value, risk, and when to stop does not.

Instrument before you scale

If you cannot measure quality on the workflow that matters, you are not ready for a broad rollout.

Keep commercial scope honest

Fixed-scope pilots beat open-ended “AI transformation” retainers when trust is still being built.
