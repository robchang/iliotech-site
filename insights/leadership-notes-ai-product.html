Leadership notes for AI product teams

Rob Chang · November 1, 2026

Leading AI product work is less about clever prompts and more about what always mattered: clear outcomes, honest scope, and a team that learns from production.

Hire for judgment

Models change every few weeks. Good judgment about user value, risk, and when to stop doesn’t.

Measure before you scale

If you can’t measure quality on the workflow that matters, you’re not ready for a broad rollout.

Keep scope honest

While trust is still being built, a fixed-scope pilot beats an open-ended “AI transformation” retainer.

Talk about a fractional engagement
